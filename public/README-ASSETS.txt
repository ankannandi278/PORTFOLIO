Drop static files here that should be served as-is (no bundling needed):

  profile.jpg     -> your profile photo (see src/data/profile.js)
  resume.pdf      -> your resume (see src/data/profile.js: resumeAvailable)
  og-cover.png    -> a 1200x630 social preview image (see index.html)
  projects/*.png  -> project screenshots (see src/data/projects.js)

Everything in this folder is publicly accessible at the site root, e.g.
public/resume.pdf becomes https://yourdomain.com/resume.pdf
